## What the evidence establishes

- Published offers, purchase routes and delivery obligations are **documented**.
  They are not executed checkouts, functional payment receipts or accepted work.
- The older public checkout reconciliation is explicitly a dated, limited report;
  it is not a current account-wide revenue measurement or evidence of buyers.
- Public product presence establishes a discovery surface at most. A URL directory
  explicitly limits its checks to URL/host/title evidence. Its historical checks
  do not establish current production behavior.
- Existing service relationships, application records and repository activity
  must not be turned into current MRR, qualified demand or product conversions.
- Unknown demand, delivery effort, margin, paid conversions and renewals stay
  unknown. This snapshot contains no current provider or end-to-end receipt.

No private payment read, purchase, customer contact, form submission, deployment,
SKU creation, billing change or automation change is performed by this app.
It retains the static CSP and makes no runtime network requests. Filters live
only in browser memory.

## Decision method

Keep the original eight dimensions and weights from #21. Every numeric research
assessment is explicitly a low-confidence **analyst estimate**, with a rationale
and supporting context. A source does not mechanically dictate the number.
Unassessed dimensions are `null`, not zero and not a pessimistic point score.

For an incomplete record, the possible weighted range uses 1 and 5 for each
unknown dimension. This is a sensitivity bound, not a statistical confidence
interval or expected revenue forecast. It conveys uncertainty rather than
rewarding missing data with an optimistic total. No midpoint is calculated, and
the UI does not sort by score or by evidence coverage. Complete records agree
with the contributor's existing scorer.

The provisional validation sequence is separate from scores:

1. **Studio services:** requalify a buyer and bound a deliverable using existing
   capability; avoid waiting for a new platform. Capacity and scope are gates.
2. **Website Intelligence:** rehearse one existing diagnostic money path, then
   measure labor and a monitoring follow-on. Distinct audit SKUs remain distinct.
3. **Digital products:** verify delivery of one useful existing kit before adding
   catalog breadth. A download sale is one-time revenue, not MRR.
4. **GoViral:** verify one accepted clip workflow, provider cost and entitlement
   lifecycle before a creator subscription pilot.
5. **DraftADynasty:** verify current data and one web premium access path; native
   store acceptance remains a separate provider-controlled lane.

This sequence is a falsifiable commercialization hypothesis, not proof that these
are the five highest expected-value businesses. No current demand or margin data
supports that stronger conclusion. Qualification results can change the sequence.
MCP Hub / Remote, Publisher and Crawler Mesh remain visible as recurring
foundations; they are not discarded because their activation evidence is missing.

## Paid-proof gate and next update

Every record includes discovery, pricing, checkout, payment, signed event, saved
order, intake, fulfillment, delivery and recovery states. A document can support
`documented`; it cannot support `verified`. The snapshot validator rejects that
promotion. Historical reports do not override missing current functional receipts.

Before marking an offer commercially verified, the owning private workflow needs
the intended product/price/environment, a successful payment and signed event,
idempotent order state, authorized intake, an accepted deliverable, delivery
receipt and reconciled refund/cancel/retry handling. Use a provider-safe test
rehearsal first. Test/operator transactions remain separate from genuine customer
demand. A subsequent renewal is separate retention evidence.

For a new assessment:

1. Reuse the existing asset/issue lane and verify visibility before collecting data.
2. Retain the exact source revision/event date, observation time and scope. Include
   failed or unavailable reads without interpreting them as a product outage.
3. Write the narrow fact proved, its limitations and the smallest missing gate.
4. Change a score only with an explanation; retain unknowns and estimate labels.
5. Submit the source/data/test update through the existing PR process. Actual
   provider receipts require a separately reviewed evidence-mode/schema extension;
   do not relax the document-only gate or publish private receipts.

Do not contact customers, buy platform credits or automatically execute work from
the validation sequence. Existing scope, customer authorization, custody, release
and spending rules still apply.
